# nanti.

Save what you want. Decide when to buy.

Nanti is a wishlist and purchase-planning app focused on helping people organize products, compare options, and make considered buying decisions.

## Product principles

- Save first, analyze later.
- Put the user's needs ahead of monetization.
- Keep every interaction simple, accessible, and reversible.
- Explain recommendations, assumptions, and research sources.
- Never let affiliate commissions influence purchase intelligence.

## Planned stack

React, Vite, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Express, Prisma, TiDB Cloud, and Vercel.

Development begins with product import, collections, wishlists, and reliable cost calculation. See `docs/product-brief.md` for the MVP scope once added.
